Membership Survey

This survey is intended to provide an overall assessment how BCCRS is meeting the social, educational and self-actualization needs of our volunteer members. It is anonymous by design. You have to be signed into your squad email to file a response but your email address will not be collected. You will, however, have the option of entering it, especially if you wish to be contacted by a member of the retention committee. Survey results will be shared with the membership when the survey is closed.
